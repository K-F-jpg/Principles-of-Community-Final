why?

Our lectures on Cultural Identity and the Sensoy & DiAngelo reading inspired me to create this project. Specific to the reading, reading about how a woman with unshaved arms would be looked at differently from a man with the same really spoke to me; although I had heard of the concept before, it got me thinking about ways I implemented this “shaved arm” ideology into my own life, enabling me to start questioning my own actions and behaviors and how they were societally influenced.

In this project, I wanted to explore how the truths we are taught when young influence our self-perception and the actions we take to adhere to the “norm”. This piece depicts societal gender norms, presenting them as they are and also providing insight into how they have evolved and changed with the times. I have two sections in this presentation: one for women (titled “Girls” for a more effeminate, youthful vibe) and one for guys, titled MEN’S- conveying masculine energy. Both present similar ideas in a different fashion, showcasing how, although we are targeted in unique ways, society demands many of the same expectations of us regardless of gender- but the way we are targeted is markedly different depending on our gender, as well. I implemented some societal norms from overseas, as well (such as South Korean hyperfixation on plastic surgery to maintain and achieve an “ideal” look), but these examples were mainly compiled from my experiences as a woman, and lifetime observer of societal expectations imposed on both men and women alike.
