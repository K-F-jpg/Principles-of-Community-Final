The lectures on Cultural Identity and Sensoy & DiAngelo reading inspired me to create this project. Specific to the reading, encountering the excerpt about how a woman with unshaved arms would be perceived differently from a man touting the same really spoke to me; although I had heard of the concept before, it got me thinking about ways I implemented this “shaved arm” ideology into my own life, leading me to question how my actions were influenced by societal standards.

In this project, I wanted to explore how our societal truths influence our self-perception and the actions we take to adhere to the “norm”. This piece depicts societal gender norms, mainly focusing on their role and evolution in the US. I included two sections in this presentation: one for women (titled “Girls” for a more effeminate, youthful vibe) and one for guys, titled MEN’S- conveying more masculine energy. Both present similar ideas in a different fashion, showcasing how, although we are targeted in unique ways, society demands many of the same expectations of us regardless of gender.
